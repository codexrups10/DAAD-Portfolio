Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for admission?

You can apply online through our admissions portal. The process takes about 15-20 minutes and requires basic personal information and academic records.

What are the admission requirements?

Requirements vary by program, but generally include high school transcripts, standardized test scores, letters of recommendation, and a personal statement.

When is the application deadline?

Fall semester applications are due March 15, and spring semester applications are due November 1. Some programs have rolling admissions.

Do you offer financial aid?

Yes, we offer various financial aid options including scholarships, grants, work-study programs, and student loans. Contact our financial aid office for details.

Can I visit the campus?

Absolutely! We offer guided campus tours throughout the week. Please schedule your visit through our website or contact the admissions office.

What is the student-to-faculty ratio?

Our student-to-faculty ratio is 15:1, ensuring that students receive personalized attention and support from our dedicated faculty members.
