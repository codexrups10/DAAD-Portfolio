Admission Requirements

Everything you need to know about applying to Elite University

Academic Excellence

High school diploma or equivalent with a minimum GPA of 3.0. Strong academic performance in core subjects is essential.

Application Form

Complete our comprehensive online application form with personal details, educational background, and program preferences.

English Proficiency

Demonstrate English language proficiency through TOEFL, IELTS, or equivalent tests for international applicants.

Application Fee

A non-refundable application fee of $50 is required to process your admission application.

Letters of Recommendation

Submit two letters of recommendation from teachers or counselors who can speak to your academic abilities.

Personal Statement

Write a 500-word personal statement explaining your goals, interests, and why you want to attend Elite University.

Application Process

Follow these simple steps to complete your application

1

Submit Application

Fill out the online application form with your personal and academic information.

2

Document Review

Our admissions team reviews your application and supporting documents.

3

Interview

Selected candidates may be invited for a personal or virtual interview.

4

Decision

Receive your admission decision within 2-4 weeks of completing your application.
