Our Heritage

Flavours Passed
Down Through Generations

Tiffin Tales was born from a deep reverence for South Indian culinary traditions. Every recipe traces its roots to ancestral kitchens in Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh — where food is not just sustenance, but a ritual of love.

We use stone-ground batters, sun-dried spices, and slow-cooked gravies that honour the original flavours. No shortcuts, no compromises — just honest, beautiful food.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

What makes your dosa batter different? +
Our batter is ground fresh daily on traditional wet-stone grinders, not blenders. This creates a finer texture and allows proper fermentation over 12–16 hours, resulting in dosas that are lighter, crisper and more flavourful.
Do you cater to vegan guests? +
Absolutely. South Indian cuisine is naturally vegan-friendly. Over 80% of our menu is vegan or easily adapted. We clearly mark all dairy-containing items. Even our ghee dishes can often be substituted with coconut oil on request.
How spicy is the food? Can I request milder versions? +
Heat levels vary by dish — we mark spicy dishes with a chilli icon. Most dishes can be prepared milder on request. Just inform your server and our chefs will adjust accordingly without compromising the flavour profile.
Do you offer South Indian thali / meal plates? +
Yes! We offer rotating regional thalis — Tamil Tiffin, Kerala Sadya, Chettinad Thali, Andhra Bhojanam and Karnataka Oota. These change with the season and festival calendar. Check our Specials section for availability.
Can I order for corporate events or large gatherings? +
Yes, we take catering orders for 20 to 500 guests including banana leaf meal services, tiffin box sets and live dosa stations. Contact us via the reservation form or call ahead at least 72 hours in advance.
