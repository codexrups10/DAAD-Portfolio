Frequently Asked Questions

📌 What is the age criteria for Nursery admission?

Children must be at least 3 years old as on 1st April of the admission year.

📌 Do you offer scholarships?

Yes, we provide scholarships to meritorious students and those excelling in sports or extracurricular activities.

📌 What documents are required for admission?

Birth certificate, previous school report card (if applicable), passport size photos, and ID proof of parents.

📌 How can I track my child’s progress?

Parents can attend PTMs (Parent-Teacher Meetings) and also access reports through our online portal.
