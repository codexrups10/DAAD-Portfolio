About Madhyamgram Girls’ High School



Empowering Girls, Shaping Futures

Welcome to Our School

At Madhyamgram Girls’ High School, we believe in the power of education to transform lives.

Madhyamgram Girls’ High School is dedicated to nurturing young minds with a commitment to academic excellence, discipline, and holistic development. Established with the vision of empowering girls through education, we provide a safe, inclusive, and intellectually stimulating environment that encourages every student to achieve her highest potential.

Our experienced faculty, well-equipped classrooms, modern laboratories, and a rich library ensure that learning is both engaging and meaningful. Beyond academics, we emphasize co-curricular activities to develop leadership, creativity, and confidence among our students.

Our Mission

To empower girls through quality education, instilling values, knowledge, and confidence that prepare them for life’s challenges.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution where every girl discovers her potential, learns with joy, and grows into a responsible global citizen.

Principal’s Message

Dear Parents and Students,
At Madhyamgram Girls’ High School, we believe that education goes beyond textbooks. Our goal is to shape young minds into compassionate, confident, and capable individuals who can contribute positively to society. We emphasize discipline, creativity, and values that prepare our students for the future.

– Principal

Our Faculty

The faculty of Madhyamgram High School is a dedicated and highly qualified team committed to providing quality education and holistic development for every student. Each member of the teaching staff brings deep subject knowledge, modern teaching methodologies, and a genuine passion for nurturing young minds. The faculty emphasizes not only academic excellence but also the moral and emotional growth of students, ensuring they are well-prepared for future challenges. Through continuous professional development, innovative classroom practices, and a supportive learning environment, the teachers of Madhyamgram High School have played a key role in maintaining the school’s reputation for excellence and integrity in education.

– Board Members

Our Legacy

For decades, Madhyamgram Girls’ High School has been a pillar of education in our community. We have produced confident, responsible, and accomplished young women who continue to excel in diverse fields. Our alumni network is a testament to the values and skills our school has imparted over the years.
